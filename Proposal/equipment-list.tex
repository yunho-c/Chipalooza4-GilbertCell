\subsection*{Equipment Checklist}

\textbf{Power and IF voltage.}
Mixer-only power requires an independently measurable supply path on the harness board.
IF gain requires a high-impedance probe with known loading, or a characterized interface to the \(50\,\Omega\) analyzer.

\textbf{RF and LO drive.}
The test uses 900~MHz RF and 890~MHz LO simultaneously.
Confirm two independently adjustable sources and either a calibrated external balun for the \(100\,\Omega\) differential RF input or a differential source.

\textbf{RF matching.}
Confirm the E5063A has a frequency option covering 900~MHz and a two-port calibration kit.
Option 205 ends at 500~MHz. Option 215 reaches 1.5~GHz.
\href{https://helpfiles.keysight.com/csg/e5063a/product_information/options.htm}{Keysight option guide}

\textbf{Two-tone linearity (IIP3).}
Requires two clean RF tones and an independent LO.
Confirm a multitone source or two RF sources with a combiner and adequate isolation.

\textbf{Single-sideband noise figure.}
Confirm a calibrated noise source or equivalent calibrated setup and a suitable IF noise receiver.
Calibration must account for receiver noise, image-band response, and RF fixture loss and imbalance.

\textbf{Temperature sweeps.}
Initial measurements are planned at ambient temperature.
Powered sweeps require a calibrated chamber or thermal stage, temperature monitoring, and RF connections to the device.
