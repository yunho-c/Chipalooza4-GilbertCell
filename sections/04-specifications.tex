Final feasibility will be assessed through SKY130 transistor-level simulations and review of the RF pad interface.

Voltage conversion gain is defined as \(20\log_{10}(V_{\mathrm{IF,rms}}/V_{\mathrm{RF,diff,rms}})\), using the single-ended IF voltage at the selected shared measurement pad and the differential voltage between \texttt{RF\_P} and \texttt{RF\_N}, with the specified match and high-impedance IF load.
The gain includes the mixer core, output interface, and selected shared output path, with the on-chip LO balun driven under specified conditions.
RF input power for IIP3 is the per-tone available power of the differential source at the pad pair; external RF fixture loss is de-embedded.
LO-to-RF isolation will be measured at both RF pads and reported for the worse result.
